# Depot System Overview

*System overview — for management review*
*Prepared 31 August 2026 · Covers 12 feature modules across the platform*

A plain-language tour of what the Depot Tracking platform does today — how depots, staff, brands, and performance are managed, and what keeps the network visible day to day.

**At a glance:** 12 feature modules · 6 bulk Excel imports · 4 user access levels · 24/7 automated monitoring

---

## Access & Security
*Who gets in, and what they can do*

**Secure sign-in**
Every user logs in with a protected account. Sessions use tokens that expire and refresh automatically, and suspicious or automated login attempts are blocked before they reach the system.

**User & role management**
Admins control access through four permission levels — Admin, Manager, Staff, Viewer. New accounts can be added one at a time or uploaded in bulk from an Excel sheet.

---

## Core Operations
*The depot network, staff, and brands*

**Depot directory**
A complete, searchable record of every depot — location, contact details, status (active, inactive, vacant, expired), owner photo, and which employee and manager oversee it. Depots can be added one at a time or imported in bulk from Excel, with a preview step that catches errors before anything is saved.

**Employee records**
Full staff profiles — department, position, contact details, and photo — plus a view of every depot each employee is responsible for. Supports bulk onboarding by Excel upload.

**Manager directory**
A dedicated roster of depot managers, each with a photo and phone number, linked directly to the depots they run.

**Brand management**
Tracks every brand operating across the depot network, including which depots carry each brand and how many.

**Province & district coverage**
Maintains the full geographic hierarchy the network operates in, importable in bulk so new regions can be added quickly as the business expands.

---

## Performance & Reporting
*Tracking results and sharing them*

**KPI performance tracking**
Monthly performance is tracked per employee, depot, and brand — purchase orders vs. target, product availability %, and shelf/volume display %. Results appear as leaderboards, summary cards, and brand/depot dashboards, or can be imported straight from monthly Excel scorecards.

**Reports & exports**
Generates polished PDF and Excel reports on demand — for depots, KPIs, and brand performance — ready to share with stakeholders.

---

## Communication
*Getting updates to the people who need them*

**Telegram alerts**
Scheduled and on-demand performance reports are delivered automatically to designated Telegram groups, so managers get updates without logging in. Delivery settings are configurable by admins.

---

## Platform Reliability
*Keeping the system trustworthy*

**Photo & document storage**
Profile photos, depot owner photos, and brand logos are uploaded and stored reliably, with cloud backup, and are viewable directly in the system.

**Always-on monitoring**
A live health check and performance dashboard continuously watch the system, so issues are caught early rather than reported by users after the fact.
